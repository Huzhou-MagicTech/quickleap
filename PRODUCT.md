# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams making decisions together. They use QuickLeap to vote on priorities, features, or directions in a structured discussion format. Context: collaborative environments where groups need to quickly reach consensus on choices, using shared discussion threads (chains) with voting and reasoning.

## Product Purpose

QuickLeap is a platform for structured team discussions with voting. Users create "chains" — discussion threads with a topic and options — then share them via human-friendly shareKeys. Participants cast votes with optional reasoning, and session-based tracking prevents duplicate votes. Success means teams reach quick, decisive conclusions without meetings or endless threads.

## Brand Personality

Fast, clear, decisive

## Anti-references

Cluttered enterprise UIs — dense dashboards, tiny text, overwhelming data presentations that slow down decision-making. QuickLeap should never feel like a corporate tool drowning users in information.

## Design Principles

1. **Decisive by default** — Every interaction should move the user closer to a decision. No friction, no noise, no dead ends.
2. **Structured clarity** — Like Notion, information is organized with clear hierarchy and visual structure. Content speaks; decoration doesn't.
3. **Speed over delight** — Animations and flourishes exist only when they accelerate understanding. The interface gets out of the way.
4. **Opinionated simplicity** — Strong defaults, minimal configuration. The product guides teams toward effective decision-making rather than offering endless options.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility requirements mandated. Standard web usability practices apply.
